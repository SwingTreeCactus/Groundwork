Groundwork

A free, private follow-up tracker for door-to-door return visits.

Open the app

What it does

Groundwork helps you keep track of the people you meet going door to door, and helps you lay the groundwork for the next visit. It remembers what you talked about, what to discuss next time, and which days people are usually home, and it shows you each day who is due for a visit.

  • Record a visit or a not-at-home in one tap.
  • See who is overdue and who is usually home today.
  • Keep notes, a visit history and the next topic for each person.
  • Works with no signal, and installs on your phone's home screen.

Your records stay yours

Everything you enter is stored only in your own browser on your own device. There is no Groundwork server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. The people who make the app never receive your records.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Groundwork saves backups of your records to a "Groundwork" folder in your own Drive, so you can restore them on a new phone. The app uses Google's drive.file permission, which only lets it see the files it made. It cannot read anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for details.

Install it

Android: open mygroundwork.app in Chrome, then choose Install app from the Chrome menu.
iPhone: open mygroundwork.app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
