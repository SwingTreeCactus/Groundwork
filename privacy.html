Groundwork privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Groundwork is a free, private follow-up tracker for door-to-door return visits. It runs entirely in your web browser. Open the app or read about Groundwork.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (names, addresses, notes, visit history, settings) is stored only in your own browser on your own device. There is no Groundwork server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. The people who make the app never receive your records.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Connect Google Drive", the app asks Google for one permission: drive.file. This lets the app create a folder named "Groundwork" in your own Google Drive and keep its backup files there: the current backup, "Groundwork backup.json", and one dated copy per day for the last 7 days (older copies are moved to your Drive bin). The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. Backups are sent directly from your browser to Google. They are never sent anywhere else, and it is not shared with the people who make the app.

The app keeps Google's sign-in tokens in your browser only so it can save the backup, including a renewal token that keeps the backup connected without signing in again every hour. To get and renew those tokens, the app passes them through a small sign-in helper run for Groundwork on Cloudflare, which holds the app's Google client secret. The helper only forwards the request to Google and hands the answer back; it does not store or log tokens, and never sees your backups or records. You can disconnect at any time in Settings > Data and backup, which revokes the tokens. The Groundwork folder and its backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Note cleanup with Claude (optional)

Note cleanup: a Clean up button appears under the fields where you write about a visit or a person (the visit or study note, household notes, Discuss next time). Only when you tap it, the text of that field (and the Discuss next time field, where there is one) is sent to Anthropic's API so Claude can tidy it, and the cleaned text comes back to the form. No other record, name list or Drive data is sent. By default the text passes through Groundwork's small helper server (a Cloudflare Worker), which adds Groundwork's API key and forwards it; the helper keeps no note text and logs nothing. So that one person can't use up the shared allowance, the helper counts each device's cleanups per day under an anonymous random ID made on that device; the count expires after two days and is not linked to your name or records. If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings > Note cleanup, the text goes directly from your browser to Anthropic using your key instead; the key is kept in your browser on that device only and is not included in backups, exports or Drive copies. Anthropic handles the text under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Hand over: when you hand a contact to another publisher, Groundwork puts that contact (name, address, area, and the notes, visits, plan and phone you choose to include) and your name and note if you add them into a link, or a file if you choose. The contact is packed into the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never send to any server, so it does not pass through Groundwork or any server of ours; you send the link yourself, through any app you choose. Anyone who has the link (or file) can open it, so send it only to the person it is for. When someone opens it, the contact is added on their device only.

Ideas for next time: on a record with nothing under Discuss next time, an Ideas button can suggest three topics from your topic list. Only when you tap it, that record's household notes, its five most recent visit notes, its stage and the last topic used (not the name, address or phone), together with your topic list, are sent to Anthropic's API the same way as Note cleanup. The three suggestions are kept on that record, on your device and in your own backups, until the next visit.

Import from a notes app, and Fill from a note when adding someone: when you choose notes (text you paste, screenshots you pick, files, or notes you share to Groundwork) and tap Make contacts or Paste, their text or the screenshots (shrunk first) are sent to Anthropic's API the same way as Note cleanup, so Claude can find the people, addresses and visits in them. Screenshots are not stored by Groundwork or its helper. The suggested contacts come back to your device for you to check; nothing is saved until you tap Import. Notes shared to Groundwork from another app wait on your device until the import screen opens, then are removed from that waiting place.

Follow-up reminders (optional)

If you turn on follow-up reminders (Menu > Notifications), your phone sends Groundwork's small reminders helper (a Cloudflare Worker) the dates on which you have a follow-up, the reminder time and your time zone, under an anonymous random ID made on your phone, together with the push address your browser gives it. No names, addresses or notes are sent. On those days the helper sends a push with no names in it; your phone then writes the reminder ("Follow-up today: Maria Lopez") from its own records. Turning reminders off deletes your entry from the helper.

The service time reminder works the same way: on the morning after a day with visits recorded but no time, the helper sends a push with no details, and your phone checks its own records before showing "Any time from yesterday to add?". Only the date goes to the helper.

Service time and reports

The time you record in the Calendar, your schedule, report comments and whether a report was sent are kept on your device with your other records, and go into your own backups and exports like everything else. Copy report puts the report's text on your phone's clipboard for you to send; Groundwork sends it nowhere. Service time is never included when you hand a contact to another publisher. The service year in review is put together on your phone from your own records and shows no names; Share makes an image on your phone that you choose where to send. Its music is fetched from this site the first time you turn it on, and nothing is sent with that request.

Imports

Importing from Ministry Assistant, Witness Work, a spreadsheet or a Groundwork backup reads the file on your device only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Links to jw.org

Topics and tracts link to jw.org and JW Library (verses, videos, tracts, workbook pages). Groundwork copies no text from jw.org and reads nothing from it; a link is only opened when you tap it.

Geotag (optional)

When you tap Geotag on a record, the app asks your phone for its location and saves the coordinates on that record (on your device and in your own backups). If you are online, the coordinates alone (no name or other details) are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to suggest a street address, which you can edit before saving. OpenStreetMap handles the request under its own privacy policy. Delete the coordinates from the record's Edit form at any time.

Map

The map in Contacts draws streets from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data), so the map squares for the area you look at are requested from their servers. Records with a geotag are placed at their coordinates on your device. For records without one, the saved street address alone (no name or notes) is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service once to find where it is; the result is saved on that record and looked up again only if the address changes. This happens when the map is shown (Contacts opens on the map once you have contacts) or when you draw areas on it. Area shapes you draw stay on your device and in your own backups.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Groundwork's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses Google data only to save and restore your own backups.

Deleting your data

Use Menu > Backup and import > Erase all data to remove everything from your device. Delete the Groundwork folder from your Google Drive to remove the backups there. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sensitive information

Records you keep may include personal details about other people. Keep them only as appropriate, protect your device with a lock, and consider the PIN lock in Settings.
