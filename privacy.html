Groundwork privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Groundwork is a free, private follow-up tracker for door-to-door return visits. It runs entirely in your web browser. Open the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (names, addresses, notes, visit history, settings) is stored only in your own browser on your own device. There is no Groundwork server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. The people who make the app never receive your records.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Connect Google Drive", the app asks Google for one permission: drive.file. This lets the app create and update a single backup file named "Groundwork backup.json" in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. The backup file is sent directly from your browser to Google. It is never sent anywhere else, and it is not shared with the people who make the app.

The app keeps Google's sign-in token in your browser only so it can save the backup. You can disconnect at any time in Settings > Data and backup, which revokes the token. The backup file stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Groundwork's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses Google data only to save and restore your own backup file.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Data and backup > Erase all data to remove everything from your device. Delete the backup file from your Google Drive to remove it there. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sensitive information

Records you keep may include personal details about other people. Keep them only as appropriate, protect your device with a lock, and consider the PIN lock in Settings.
