# Groundwork: UX and workflow audit (2026-11-03, at v2.50.2)

Status: review only, nothing built. Done by walking every view at phone size twice, as a new user and as
someone with 16 contacts and three months of visits, Not Homes and studies, and reading the code behind
what looked wrong. Measured against the mission as it now reads on the first view, and against the model
games already named in the design doc (Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing, Duolingo, Apple Fitness, Pokémon GO,
Neko Atsume, Viridi, Finch). Companion to claude/app-audit.md (2026-10-31), whose open items are listed at
the end.

## The mission, as the yardstick

"A garden that keeps your return visits from falling through the cracks." Then: your conversation topics
are ready for the day; your records stay private on your phone, with a Drive backup. So three tests:

1. Does the app make sure nobody is forgotten, and does the garden drive that?
2. Does it get you ready for the day?
3. Is your data safe?

## Verdict

Preparation is excellent and private-by-design is real. The garden is beautiful and calm. The gap is in
the first and most important test: **the garden shows that people need you, but never who, and never lets
you act on it.** Seeing, choosing and doing are spread over three tabs. The model games all close that loop
in one place: in Stardew you walk to the dry crop and water it; Duolingo opens on the next lesson's button;
Apple Fitness's ring is the goal and the progress at once; Pokémon GO's map is where to go. Groundwork's
watering can says "7 left to water" and stops. Fix that one loop and the rest of the game starts working
for the mission instead of beside it.

## What works

- **Today prepares you.** The campaign bar, the tract carousel (main scripture, Enjoy Life lesson), and "If
  you see them" with what to bring. Nothing else in the ministry-app space does this.
- **The record screen** leads with the plan, the last visit and one main action; Not Home is one tap.
- **The visit sheet** is quick: topic chips, Clean up, Discuss next time, the study switch.
- **The garden is a place you want to open**: time of day, seasons, visitors, nothing dies, no care words.
- **The new first view** says what the app is, and setup can be finished in one sitting.
- No console errors on any view walked.

## Findings, most important first

### High: the core loop

1. **The garden never says who needs water.** The can sheet gives "0/7 · 7 left to water"; the bed signs
   have a clay dot; a plant has to be tapped one at a time to learn its name. Today shows people likely home,
   not people going thirsty, and "14 people to see today" (14 of 16 contacts) opens Contacts in its last view.
   So the one question the mission promises to answer, "who am I about to lose?", has no direct answer.
   Model: Stardew's dry soil you walk up to; Animal Crossing's villager with a "!" bubble. Fix: the watering
   can sheet lists the plants left to water this week by name, grouped by area, likely-home first, each
   tapping straight to Record a visit or Not Home, and a quiet line under the carousel on Today ("3 plants
   left to water this week") opening the same list. No new screen, no count badge, no red.

2. **A plan is counted even when nobody wrote one.** Record a visit pre-fills Discuss next time with the
   last plan, and the sheet then says "Plan set · lush". Saving without touching it marks the visit as
   planned (`entry.plan` is just "the field isn't empty"), so lush plants, the "Leave a plan" tending goal
   and the bees all reward an old plan carried forward. The quality lever the game was built around is on by
   default. Fix: count a plan only when Discuss next time was written or changed in this visit (or a
   topic's linking question was picked); show the carried-over plan as the thing to cover, with the field
   empty for the next one.

3. **Evening visits show the wrong day.** `U.fmtDate` reads the date part of the UTC timestamp, so a study
   logged at 6:15 pm in California says "last covered Lesson 4, Tue, Oct 27" for a Monday study (screens.js,
   the Studying line and one other `fmtDate(x.at)`). Studies and return visits are mostly evenings. Fix:
   format timestamps in local time.

### Medium: the game's signals

4. **Two buntings mean two things.** The streak is pennants on the fence (one per good week), and a running
   campaign hangs coloured bunting too. With a streak of 0 and a campaign on, the fence looks fully
   decorated while the Streak sheet says 0. Fix: keep bunting for the streak only; mark a campaign another
   way (a banner on the gate, or nothing in the garden; it already leads Today).

5. **Progress can vanish without a word.** A growth bar resets to empty when the chain gap passes (the
   person goes un-visited past the stage window). That's the one place "nothing earned is ever lost" isn't
   true, and nothing says why. Model: Duolingo's streak repair, Finch's gentle "welcome back". Fix: keep
   the steps and let only the plant's care fade, or show the reset once ("Started again: it's been a while").

6. **Thirst is hard to read at small sizes.** In Areas, three thumbnail pots per area are the only sign;
   at that size wilting reads as an empty pot. Areas are sorted A to Z, not by who needs you. Fix: sort areas
   with thirsty plants first, and add a plain count beside the pots ("3 to water"), the one place a number
   helps more than a picture.

7. **Many systems, little to do in the garden.** Five forms, four care levels, ten kinds, flowers, fruit
   buds, 15 visitors and rare coats, 20 milestones, streak and freezes, weekly can, three tending goals, the
   round and weekly recaps, the field guide. The models keep one daily loop and let collection sit around it.
   Groundwork's daily loop (water the thirsty) has no place to happen in the garden, so the collections carry
   the weight. Fix 1 above gives the loop a home; after that, hold new systems (the shelved XP and shop stay
   shelved) and consider folding the can, slate and bunting into one "This week" sheet.

### Medium: views and workflow

8. **Today's carousel card is half empty.** The tract card is ~380 px tall with the question at the top and
   the buttons at the bottom, about 200 px of blank card between. "If you see them" only just makes the first
   screen and the "people to see today" line sits under the + button. Fix: size the card to its content
   (title, question, scripture, lesson).

9. **A study keeps an old Discuss next time.** Patricia's record (Bible study, Lesson 5 next) still shows
   "Discuss next time: Psalm 37:29" from before the study began. Fix: hide Discuss next time on a study
   record when it predates the first study session (keep it in the timeline).

10. **The timeline is noisy.** Every entry carries Edit and Delete, and the newest entry repeats the Last
    visit block above it. Fix: tap an entry to edit (Delete inside the sheet), as the Last visit block
    already does.

11. **"Home" tab, "Today" title.** The first tab is labelled Home but its screen is titled Today. Fix: name
    the tab Today.

12. **A new user's Contacts opens on an empty map.** "Geotag an RV or add addresses to see them here."
    Fix: with no contacts, open on List with Add and the two import rows; and "contact", not "RV", in empty
    states.

13. **"Contact" and "RV" are still mixed** (open since the last audit): Edit RV, Delete this RV, "the
    not-home banner for this RV", "16 RVs and 38 visits on this phone", and the Welcome card's "Add your
    first RV" (added in v2.50.1). Keep RV only as the stage name.

### Low

14. A contact with no visits shows "last visit yesterday" (its creation date) in Areas.
15. The grapevine's arbour runs past the left edge of the record card.
16. The Streak sheet leads with a large clay "0". Calm, but it is the one place the game shows a zero big;
    "Your first good week will hang a pennant here" says the same thing forward.

## Still open from the 2026-10-31 audit

- Backup reminder and a last-backup date (data loss is still one cleared browser away; only Getting started
  and "Not backed up yet" on the Backup page say so).
- The menu ignores Back and Escape.
- The privacy page still says the map is on Insights and that nothing is sent until you open it.
- 14 test screenshots and the old /rv-app/ are still deployed at the site root.
- "N people to see today" goes to Contacts' last view, not those people (folded into finding 1).
- Small tap targets (A to Z strip, bed signs).
- The Insights lists (Worth reconsidering, Stuck in stage) are still unreachable with the garden on.

## Suggested order

1. The loop: names in the watering can sheet and a Today line to it (1), plans that count only when written
   (2), local dates (3).
2. Signals: one meaning for bunting (4), say when progress restarts (5), areas sorted and counted (6).
3. Views: carousel card height (8), study plan (9), timeline (10), Today tab (11), empty Contacts (12),
   wording (13), and the backup reminder from the last audit.
4. Hold new game systems until the loop has been used for a few weeks (7).
