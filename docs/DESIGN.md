## 68. v2.53.0 The Calendar: service time, the report, scheduled visits (built 2026-11-06)

Release 1 of 3 agreed with Mark on 2026-11-06 (design: docs/SERVICE-TIME.md, project claude/service-time.md; mockups: design canvas "Groundwork Calendar", https://claude.ai/artifact/Ugp6FJpyAg6QPXNFjfiKdg). Release 2 is the year (year view, past months, the year in review with music); release 3 the game toggles and the Ideas switch.
- **Fourth tab, Calendar** (`#/calendar`, `#/calendar/YYYY-MM`, `#/calendar/schedule`, `#/calendar/profile`; js/calendar.js, `RV.calendar`). Logic in js/service.js (`RV.service`, pure parts tested in Node).
- **Profiles by month** (`settings.service.profiles`, each `{from: 'YYYY-MM', kind}`): Regular pioneer (50 h, 600 h a service year Sep–Aug), Continuous auxiliary (30 h, month to month), Publisher (time tracking on or off), Other goal (its own hours). Auxiliary months (15 or 30 h) sit on a Publisher base (`settings.service.aux`) and always keep time. Past months keep their profile; the first profile chosen also covers the months before it. Badge at the top (sheet with the goal, the year's hours and credit, Edit).
- **The safety net:** a day with live visits, Not Homes or new contacts (no imports or handoffs) and no time has a clay ring, from the day tracking began, in this month and last month until its report is ticked Sent. A card lists those days (chips open them; × hides it). The day sheet lists what was recorded that day; Add session, or No service this day (grey dot, Undo). Adding time fills the ring into a green dot (`cring2dot`). No suggested spans; nothing on the tab or Today.
- **Recording time:** sessions (lib items kind `time`), each Total or Start–finish (with Break), Ministry or Credit, on vertical wheels in 15-minute steps (`vwheel`, scroll-snap, a light tick on Android); the mode last used is remembered (`rvapp.timeMode`). Overlapping sessions count the shared minutes once (the earlier session keeps them), with "Overlaps … · Fix"; a day can't pass 24 hours.
- **Totals:** a month's hours round down; leftover minutes carry into the next month, service and credit separately, worked out from the records each time. Credit counts toward the goal and shows on its own.
- **Pace card:** "11 h 15 m this month", recorded and planned fill, "On pace for 41 h" · "Goal 50 h", "Includes … credit and … carried from October." Pace = recorded + the plan for the rest of the month (today counts the larger). Goal reached: a full bar with a leaf. No hours-to-go, no behind.
- **My schedule:** one weekly pattern (`settings.service.pattern`), sessions repeating every week, every other week (from a chosen week) or chosen weeks of the month; any future day can be changed on the calendar (`overrides`). Planned hours show faintly on future days.
- **Follow-ups** (follow-up dates) show as a small person on their day; a future day's sheet lists them, with Plan a visit (picks a contact, sets the follow-up date; today's adds to Today's list).
- **Bible studies:** everyone with a study session that month; fewer than three in all shows "1st study" / "2nd study"; − and + adjust the count (studies not in Groundwork). Imported studies count here.
- **Report:** last month's row (or the month viewed): Copy (text to the clipboard), Sent checkbox (stamp on the month), comments, history. Changing a month marked Sent asks once ("…’s report is marked sent. Changing it may not match your publisher record card." Change anyway) and writes the change to the month's history; nothing is flagged. Month records are lib items kind `svmonth`, id `svm-YYYY-MM`.
- **Month look back:** the first open in a new month (after setup) shows last month: hours, days out, studies, people visited, comments, Copy report, Sent, Later.
- **Update card** for existing users, once: "New: Calendar", three lines, "How do you report?" (four choices), the auxiliary line, Not now (Publisher, time off). New users get the question on the Calendar tab. Browser tests that set `__RV_SURPRISE` never see these cards unless they set `__RV_SVCCARD = 1`.
- **Service time reminder** (Menu › Notifications, its own switch beside Follow-up reminders; one subscription, `RV.reminders.setKind`): the morning after a day with visits and no time; the service worker checks the phone's records and adds "Any time from yesterday to add?" (alone it opens the Calendar). Still only dates go to the helper. Works once the reminders helper is deployed.
- Export › Service time (CSV). Privacy page: a service time section. Service time goes in backups and exports, never in a handoff. Time and month records merge record by record; the profile and schedule live in settings.
- Not in this release: the year view and entering past months by total (release 2); importing service time from Witness Work.
- Tests: service 15 (new, node), calendar_ui 41 (new, browser), smoke 383 (four tabs), screens 60, kinds 23, areas_ui 42, reminders_ui 12, ai_cleanup 60, notes_import 39, drive_folder 37, garden 45, logic 53, others unchanged. VERSION 2.53.0, cache rvapp-v115.

## 71. v2.56.0 Anonymous usage counts (built 2026-11-06)

Mark (2026-11-06): how many people use Groundwork, and which features; free; no login. Agreed: no Google login (it would put publishers' emails on a server; Drive backup already keeps people signed in and backs up on its own), an anonymous daily check-in instead.
- **App** (js/stats.js, `RV.stats`): at most once a day (`rvapp.statsDay`), a few seconds after opening or coming back to the app, when online, it posts `{ id, v, device, mode, f }` to `RV.config.stats + '/ping'`: the install code (`RV.ai.installId()`, `rvapp.installId`, made once, kept across updates and Erase all data), the version, ios / android / desktop, installed / browser, and feature flags (garden on, Calendar set up, Drive connected, reminders on, Note cleanup used this week via `rvapp.cleanupUsed`, visits recorded this week). Nothing else. Off with the switch in Menu › About (`rvapp.statsOff`); nothing is sent while `RV.config.stats` is empty (it ships empty). About's version line, pressed and held, opens the stats page.
- **Helper** (worker/stats.js, Cloudflare Worker `groundwork-stats` with a D1 database bound as `DB`; `ALLOWED_ORIGIN`, secret `ADMIN_KEY`; a daily cron): tables made on first use: `installs (id, first, last, v, device, mode)` and `days (day, id, v, device, mode, f)`; two rows written per install per day at most. No IP addresses stored. `/ping` only from the app's site and strictly checked; `/` is the stats page (no data in it; asks for the admin key once, kept in that browser); `/api/summary` needs `Authorization: Bearer <ADMIN_KEY>` (constant-time check). The cron removes day rows older than 400 days; installs stay for "ever".
- **Stats page**: active today / this week / in 30 days, installs ever, new this week / in 30 days, bar charts of active and new installs each day for 60 days, versions, devices, installed vs browser, and features among installs active in 30 days.
- An install = one copy of the app on one device: deleting and re-adding the home-screen app, Safari plus the home-screen app on iPhone, or clearing the site's data count again; inactive copies drop out of "active" after 30 days.
- Free: Workers free plan 100,000 requests a day (shared with the other helpers); D1 free plan 100,000 rows written and 5 million read a day, 5 GB; with no card on file nothing can be charged; past a daily limit the check-ins quietly fail until midnight UTC.
- Privacy page: an "Anonymous usage counts" section.
- Tests: stats 7 (new, node, D1 played by Node's SQLite), stats_ui 14 (new, browser), others unchanged. VERSION 2.56.0, cache rvapp-v119.


## 72. v2.56.1 The year pace from the monthly average (built 2026-11-06)

Mark (2026-11-06): My schedule shouldn't be applied to the whole service year; only to the rest of this month. Beyond this month, average the time.
- **Year pace** (`RV.service.ahead`, used by `yearOf` and `yearMonths`): this month is recorded + My schedule for the rest of it (the month pace card's "On pace for", unchanged). Each later month in the service year is expected at the average of the earlier months' totals and this month's projection. Year projection = earlier months + this month's projection + average × months left.
- Months before tracking began (before `settings.service.since`, with no time in Groundwork and no total entered) are left out of the average; a total entered by hand counts, and a month after tracking began with no time counts as zero. Credit counts in the totals. In September the average is just September's projection.
- The year view looks the same: the ring and "On pace for" use the new projection; the future bars' light fill is the average. The key reads Recorded / Expected; the note: "Tap a month to open it. This month uses My schedule; later months use your monthly average."

## 74. v2.56.3 The garden visit stays put, and moments swipe away (built 2026-11-11)

Mark (2026-11-11): "See it in your garden" scrolled down to the visitor, bounced back to the top, then scrolled down again; and he couldn't swipe the visitor announcement away. Swipe direction should follow where a notice sits: sideways or down for anything full-screen, centred or low, up for a notice at the top of the screen.
- **The bounce.** The focus link `#/insights/focus/<visitor>` swaps itself for `#/insights` while the garden draws, but the router remembered the old address as the page it was on. The first redraw after that (usually the save marking the visitor seen, about three seconds in, once the garden's one-off saves for milestones and settling visitors were already done) took `#/insights` for a different page and restored that page's old scroll position, usually the top; the still-pending focus request then scrolled down again. Now the router records the address as it stands after drawing (`lastHash = location.hash`), so a redraw keeps the scroll; and the focus only scrolls when the visitor is outside the screen (90 px margins), so a redraw part way doesn't nudge it again.
- **`RV.swipe.away(box, close, {up, scroller})`** (js/swipe.js): sideways either way, or vertically toward the nearest screen edge (down by default, up with `up: true`); vertical only once the scroller is at that end, so long content still scrolls; a short swipe springs back; buttons all stay. Touch runs on touch events (non-passive touchmove) so the drag can stop the page scrolling on iPhone; mouse and pen use pointer events. The whole overlay moves, so the app shows beneath as it goes.
- Uses: the visitor announcement (= Later; replaces its v2.55 pull-down), the weekly recap (= Close), the round (= Close). All three fill the screen, so they go down or sideways. Nothing in the app sits at the top of the screen today; `up` is there for the first notice that does.
