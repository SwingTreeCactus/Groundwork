# Groundwork: app audit (2026-10-31, at v2.45.0)

Status: review only, nothing built. Done by walking every main screen at phone size with realistic data,
reading the code behind them, and checking the live pages, against what Groundwork is for: help a publisher
go back to the people they meet (prepare, find them home, remember what was said, keep at it), privately.

## Verdict

The core is strong and specific. Today prepares you, a record tells you what to bring, the visit sheet is
quick, Clean up turns rough notes into usable ones, and the garden makes coming back feel good. No other
ministry tool does preparation, likely-home timing or the garden. The weak spots are not features. They are
data safety, a few loose ends left by fast iteration (orphaned screens, stale wording, an out-of-date
privacy page, files that shouldn't be public), and a game that now has many systems.

## What works

- Today is focused: campaign, one topic carousel, who to see. It prepares rather than lists.
- The record screen leads with what matters (plan, last visit, topic and next part) and one main action.
- Entry is fast: chips, Clean up, the full-screen note, tending nudges.
- Imports cover what people actually have (Ministry Assistant, Witness Work, notes apps, CSV, backups).
- Privacy by design: records on the device, Drive only with drive.file, helpers that store nothing.
- About 700 automated checks across 13 suites, all passing; no console errors on any screen walked.

## Issues, most important first

### High

1. **Records can be lost.** Everything lives in the browser's storage on one phone. Unless Drive is
   connected, nothing reminds you to back up: the Getting started step can be skipped, and after that the
   only backup notice is "Drive backup paused". On iPhone, Safari can clear storage for sites that aren't
   added to the home screen and haven't been used for a while. A lost phone or a cleared browser
   means losing every record.
   Fix: a quiet backup reminder (for example after 10 contacts or 14 days with no backup, on Today
   under the carousel, dismissible), and "Last backup: date" at the foot of the menu.

2. **The menu ignores Back and Escape.** With the menu open, the phone's Back button (or Escape)
   changes the page underneath and leaves the menu open on top; only the X or a tap on the scrim closes it.
   Fix: close the menu on route change and on Escape; push a history state when it opens so Back
   closes it.

3. **The privacy page is out of date.** It says the map is on Insights and that nothing is sent until you
   open the Map view; Contacts now opens on the map, so addresses go to OpenStreetMap the first time
   Contacts opens. It also says Clean up is "under the visit note" (it's now on every visit and person
   field) and doesn't mention the Witness Work import or the jw.org links on topics.
   Fix: rewrite those sections. Consider opening Contacts on Areas until the person first chooses Map,
   so nothing is sent without a choice.

4. **Files that shouldn't be public are deployed.** 18 test screenshots sit at the site root
   (01-pick-empty.png and others) and the old v1 app is still served at /rv-app/ with its own service worker.
   Fix: delete them from the repo (and add the screenshot names to .gitignore).

### Medium

5. **Orphaned features.** With the garden on (the default), Insights shows only the garden, so the funnel,
   "Worth reconsidering" and "Stuck in stage" can't be reached, while their settings ("Stuck in stage"
   timing in Attention) are still shown. "Worth reconsidering" is useful in the ministry (people to
   stop visiting or hand on).
   Fix: decide to keep or drop. If kept, put it in Contacts as a filter or a section at the foot of
   Areas, not in the garden.

6. **"7 people to see today" goes nowhere specific.** It opens Contacts in whatever view was last used,
   not those seven people.
   Fix: link to Contacts with the Today filter on (the map already has a Today chip; Areas and List
   would need it).

7. **Wording is mixed between "RV" and "contact".** "New contact" but "Edit RV", the + button is labelled
   "New RV" for screen readers, the stage shows "RV", NH buttons, "Never show the not-home banner for
   this RV". Pick one word for the person (contact) and keep RV only as the stage name.

8. **Record screen: the growth path is clipped.** The row of small pots at the top sits under the title
   bar, cut off.

9. **The game has many systems.** Five forms, four care levels, flowers, fruit buds, ten kinds and the
   reveal, fifteen visitors and rare coats, a weekly ring, a weekly streak with freezes, three tending
   goals and rain, twenty exhibits, the round recap, the weekly recap and the field guide. XP, a shop,
   items, a companion and named visitors are next. Each is calm on its own; together they are a lot to
   learn, and the "How your garden works" sheet already has to explain much of it.
   Recommendation: before adding XP, decide what it replaces. One option: exhibits become items earned
   by milestones (so milestones fill the items board), and the weekly ring and streak fold into one
   "this week" view. Fewer systems, each doing more.

10. **Getting started is dominated by imports.** On first run, five import rows fill the screen between
    "Add your first contact" and the other steps. Fix: one row, "Bring in what you have", opening the
    import chooser.

11. **Small tap targets.** The A to Z strip letters (18 x 15 px), the stage name under the person's name
    (20 x 25 px) and the bed signs in the garden (26 px tall) are under the usual 44 px minimum.

### Low and technical

12. **CSS layers.** css/app.css is about 1,900 lines with the colour tokens redefined in 21 theme blocks and
    12 rules for the retired Purple and Blue accents. Later blocks win, so it works, but every design
    change risks an old rule resurfacing. A cleanup pass (one token block per theme, dead rules removed)
    would make future changes safer.
13. **No Content-Security-Policy.** Low risk (all user text is rendered as text, not HTML), but the
    person's own API key and Drive tokens live in localStorage, so a CSP header on Cloudflare would be
    cheap insurance.
14. **Known and accepted:** an app update can reload the page during a notes import (Mark, 2026-10-30).
15. **Still to check on a phone:** verse links open JW Library at the right verse; workbook video links
    open the right video.

## Strategy

Groundwork's real neighbours are Ministry Assistant and Witness Work, not the friend-keeping apps. Its
case is that it helps you go back, not just keep records. Before more features, the most useful step is
to put it in front of a few publishers and watch what they use and where they get lost.

## Suggested order

1. Backup reminder and last-backup date (1), menu Back fix (2), remove public files (4), privacy page (3).
2. Wording pass (7), Today link (6), growth path clip (8), tap targets (11), Getting started (10).
3. Decide on the orphaned Insights lists (5) and on simplifying the game before XP (9).
4. CSS cleanup and CSP (12, 13) when there's a quiet moment.
